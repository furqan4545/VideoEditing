I made a FREE AI voice more human every level 😳 Level 6 is my own voice… and it got creepy

Levels 2 to 6 were made in VoiceStudio: free, open source, runs on YOUR laptop. Level 1 is an old-school computer voice, just to compare.

The 6 levels:
1️⃣ Old computer voice: no pauses, no feelings 🤖
2️⃣ Add punctuation. Dots… and full stops give it room to breathe
3️⃣ Switch to an emotion engine (VoxCPM2) and write how it should feel: "(nervous, stammering)"
4️⃣ Same engine, new feeling: "(offended, sassy)"
5️⃣ Type [laughter] in the text and it really laughs 😂
6️⃣ Clone your own voice from a short clip and pick it as the voice

🔗 github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio

⚡ Easiest setup: open Claude Code and paste this:
"Install the VoiceStudio app on this device and verify it works, following https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/blob/main/docs/install/agent.md"

Or install it yourself:
🍎 Mac (Apple Silicon): curl -fsSL https://voicestudio.sh/install | sh
🪟 Windows 10/11 (PowerShell): irm https://voicestudio.sh/install.ps1 | iex
🐧 Linux: same command as Mac

The emotion engines are optional downloads in the app's Model Manager.
