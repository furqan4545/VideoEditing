# Reel 5: "How human can a FREE AI voice get?" (~32 s)

**What we take from the reference reel** (Instagram DfUe1XXx6Jw) is only the format:
- a fixed title pill;
- simple captions;
- a small demo card over the chest;
- something that gets better step by step, so people stay to see how far it goes.

**The story in one line:** Ali gives a free AI voice six tries to prove it's not a robot. Every try it sounds more
human, and on the last try it uses Ali's own voice and turns the question back on him.

**The callback:** on level 1 Ali tells the AI "you are" (a robot). On level 6 the AI, in Ali's voice, says "you are"
back to him.

## On screen
- **Title pill** (fixed): `How human can a FREE AI voice get?`
- **Demo card on the chest:**
  - what was typed into VoiceStudio, exactly, with this level's change highlighted (the dots, the direction, the tag);
  - the setting name, e.g. `Emotion: nervous`;
  - a waveform that plays with the voice;
  - `LEVEL 3/6` and six dots filling up.
- **Captions:** Ali's words in the usual serif with the lit word; the AI's lines in a different colour.

## The story

**ALI:** "This free AI says it's not a robot. Let's test it."
DO: point down at the card on "This free AI". Look back into the lens and lean in on "Let's test it."
(Backups: "This AI thinks it's human. Let's find out." / "This free AI swears it's human. Watch.")

### Level 1 · The default voice
Card: `hello i am not a robot i am a real human`

**AI** *(flat, rushed, no pauses)*: "hello i am not a robot i am a real human"
DO: deadpan stare into the lens.

**ALI:** "Yeah… you are. Add some dots."
DO: poke the air on "dots".

### Level 2 · Punctuation
Card: `Hi… I'm not a robot. I promise.` (the `…` and `.` highlighted)

**AI:** "Hi… I'm not a robot. I promise."
DO: small "hm, better" nod.

**ALI:** "Better. Now make it nervous."

### Level 3 · Emotion: nervous
Card: `(nervous, stammering) Why are you staring? I'm… I'm not a robot.`

**AI** *(nervous)*: "Why are you staring? I'm… I'm not a robot."
DO: lean in and squint at it.

**ALI:** "Suspicious. Now offended."

### Level 4 · Emotion: offended
Card: `(offended, sassy) Excuse me? Robots don't get offended. And I am VERY offended.`

**AI** *(offended)*: "Excuse me? Robots don't get offended. And I am *very* offended."
DO: eyebrows up, lean back a little.

**ALI:** "Okay. Make it laugh."

### Level 5 · A real laugh
Card: `[laughter] Me? A robot? That's the funniest thing I've heard all day.`

**AI** *(laughs first)*: "Me? A robot? That's the funniest thing I've heard all day."
DO: laugh with it, then stop, a bit creeped out.

**ALI:** "Last level. Use my voice."
DO: point at yourself.

### Level 6 · Your own voice
Card: `(whispering) Relax. I'm not the robot… you are.` and `Voice: Ali (clone)`

**AI** *(Ali's voice, whispering)*: "Relax. I'm not the robot… you are."
DO: freeze. Slowly look into the lens.

**ALI:** "…am I?"
DO: hold the look for 1 second. Then, normal again:

**ALI:** "It's free. Everything's in the description."

## How I make the AI lines (all in VoiceStudio, one at a time)

| Level | Engine and setting |
|---|---|
| 1 | Default engine, typed all lowercase with no punctuation. If it already sounds too good, I keep the flattest real take. No fake robot filter. |
| 2 | Same voice, same engine, with the dots and full stops. |
| 3 | VoxCPM2 with the direction `(nervous, stammering)`. |
| 4 | VoxCPM2 with the direction `(offended, sassy)`. |
| 5 | OmniVoice with the `[laughter]` tag. |
| 6 | Ali's clone with the direction `(whispering)`. If the whisper doesn't hold, a quiet, slow read. |

Levels 1–5 keep one AI voice, so it sounds like the same character getting better. Every line is checked with
VoiceStudio's transcription before it goes in.

## Recording notes
- **Framing:** phone at eye level, with your face in the **top half** of the frame. The card covers your chest, so keep
  your hands beside your face.
- **The pauses:** after each line, **stay silent for about 3 seconds** and keep your reaction going (stare, nod, squint,
  laugh, freeze). That gap is where the AI voice plays. I trim each gap to the exact length of the AI line, so it
  never feels slow.
- **Takes:** the whole thing **3 times**, in one go each. If you trip, say the line again and keep going.
- **Sound:** a quiet room, phone close.
