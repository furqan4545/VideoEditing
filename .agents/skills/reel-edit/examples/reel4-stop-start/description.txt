Stop making AI voiceovers that sound like a robot 🤖 It's 3 settings, not the app 🎬

Everything in this video was made in VoiceStudio: free, open source, runs on YOUR laptop. Even the last line… that's my AI clone.

The 3 fixes:
1️⃣ Switch the engine. The default voice can't act. Pick an emotion engine (VoxCPM2, CosyVoice 3 or IndexTTS2) and write how it should sound: "(tired, whispering, about to cry)"
2️⃣ Clone from the right clip. It copies HOW you talk, not just your voice. A flat clip = a flat voice, so record 10 seconds in the mood you want.
3️⃣ Write for breathing. Dots… dashes — and pauses like [pause 500ms]. One long paragraph sounds like a robot reading.

⚠️ Bonus: don't paste ElevenLabs tags like [excited]. VoiceStudio reads them out loud 😭
