# Reel 4: "Stop making robotic AI voiceovers" (~40 s)

Format copied from the reference reel (Instagram DfdePZHtATL, 28 s):
1. Show the boring way.
2. Show the better way, as an instant before/after.
3. "Here's how", in 3 steps, each with a small labelled card above the head.
4. One-line takeaway.
5. Close.

Our version swaps each step into a STOP / START pair.

Every tip below is a real VoiceStudio feature (docs/expressive-speech.md in the repo):
- The emotion engines (VoxCPM2, CosyVoice 3, IndexTTS2) take a written direction.
- The reference clip sets the delivery.
- Pauses and punctuation work on every engine.
- Unknown tags like ElevenLabs' [excited] get read out loud.

---

## Take 1: Hook (before / after)

**SAY:** "Stop making AI voiceovers that sound like this…"
**DO:** Look straight into the lens. On "this", point down at the bottom of the screen.

**SAY:** "…and start making them sound like this."
**DO:** On "this", snap your fingers, or point to the other side.

## Take 2: Hand-off

**SAY:** "Same free app. Here's how."
**DO:** Hold up three fingers.

## Take 3: Step 1

**SAY:** "One. Stop using the default voice for acting. Switch to an emotion engine like VoxCPM2, and tell it how to feel: tired… whispering… about to cry."
**DO:** Hold up one finger at "One". On "tired… whispering… about to cry", act each word a little with your face.

## Take 4: Step 2

**SAY:** "Two. Stop cloning from a boring clip. It copies HOW you talk, not just your voice. So record ten seconds in the mood you want."
**DO:** Hold up two fingers at "Two". On "record", hold an imaginary phone up to your mouth.

## Take 5: Step 3

**SAY:** "Three. Stop writing one long paragraph. Add pauses and dots, and it starts breathing like a real person."
**DO:** Hold up three fingers at "Three". On "breathing", take one big, visible breath.

## Take 6: Bonus warning

**SAY:** "And stop pasting ElevenLabs tags like 'excited'. This app will literally read them out loud."
**DO:** Shake your head. On "out loud", laugh.

## Take 7: Takeaway + close

**SAY:** "Remember: it doesn't just copy your voice. It copies your acting. Link and setup are in the description."
**DO:** On "your acting", tap your chest. End on a thumbs up and hold it for 1 second.

## What I do in the edit (not you)

- **After "like this…" (take 1):**
  - The BAD take plays: your cloned voice, flat and robotic, saying "In a world where every voice costs money, one free app changed everything."
  - On screen: a red ❌ "ROBOTIC" card.
- **After "…like this." (take 1):**
  - The GOOD take plays: the same line as a slow, deep movie-trailer voice, with pauses and the music hitting under it.
  - On screen: a green ✅ "CINEMATIC" card.
- **Step cards above your head, like the reference:**
  - 1: STOP default voice → START (tired, whispering)
  - 2: STOP flat clip → START record the mood
  - 3: STOP wall of text → START … [pause]
- **Screenshots:** the Emotion button, the clone box, and a script with `[pause 500ms]` in it.
- **Bonus (take 6):** a short clip of the app actually saying "excited" out loud.
- **Throughout:** captions, music with drops, sound effects, and the slow zoom.

## For every take

- Shoot vertical, in the same spot and light as reel 3.
- Hold your hand up still for 1 second at each "One / Two / Three", so the step cards can land on it.
